Video 09 — "How to do YouTube SEO to get high ticket clients"
18 full-screen graphic scenes, rendered separately.

HOW TO USE
  Each file is named with the timecode it goes at on your basic-edit timeline:
      scene-01_01-36-15.mp4  ->  drop at 00:01:36:15
  Drop each clip on a video track above the talking head at that timecode.
  Clips are 1920x1080, 30 fps, silent, hard cut in and out. Keep your edit audio underneath.

  TIMECODES.CSV has the same information for every clip, including in/out timecode,
  in/out frame numbers, duration and frame count.

NOTES
  Scenes are numbered 01-19 with no scene 10 (dropped).
  They cover the three stretches where the talking head runs longer than 30 seconds:
      zone A  01:31 - 05:07   scenes 01-09
      zone B  19:27 - 20:19   scenes 11-14
      zone C  20:53 - 22:55   scenes 15-19
